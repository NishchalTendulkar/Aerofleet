## 🛡️ Project Defense & Evaluator Q&A Guide (Qualitative)

### Core Question: How does AeroFleet AI differ from standard predictive maintenance software?
* **Core Answer:** "Most existing tools stop at anomaly detection—they trigger an alert when a vibration or temperature threshold is exceeded. AeroFleet is **availability-aware**. It connects the diagnostic alert directly to operational constraints: checking spare parts across forward airbases, reviewing depot hangar bay capacity, evaluating pilot logbook snags, and providing maintenance crews with step-by-step procedures from parsed technical manuals. It turns raw diagnostic alarms into coordinated maintenance execution."

### Core Question: How does the Cutaway Digital Twin assist real-world technicians?
* **Core Answer:** "On the flight line, translating abstract sensor telemetry into physical inspection targets often causes delays. Our cutaway digital twin allows line personnel to view internal assemblies that are normally obscured by airframe panels. By isolating subsystems such as the propulsion core down to compressor and turbine stages, technicians immediately identify the physical location of the anomaly before loosening a single bolt."

### Core Question: How does the system handle supply chain and logistical delays?
* **Core Answer:** "A major cause of avoidable aircraft grounding is waiting for replacement parts. AeroFleet integrates inventory visibility across forward operating bases and central supply depots. When an emerging fault is detected, the platform proactively checks regional stock and facilitates immediate inter-base reallocation, ensuring the required component arrives at the operational base ahead of scheduled maintenance."

### Core Question: What role does the AI Copilot play in the maintenance workflow?
* **Core Answer:** "Aviation maintenance manuals and technical orders are dense and time-consuming to search under operational pressure. The AeroFleet AI Copilot acts as a specialized assistant that synthesizes live sensor signals, historical maintenance records, and approved maintenance documentation. Technicians can ask direct operational questions and instantly receive diagnostic reasoning, component verification, and actionable triggers to schedule maintenance tasks."
